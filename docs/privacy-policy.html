Privacy Policy

Berlaku mulai:Effective: 20 November 2026

NuFit AI ("kami") adalah aplikasi pendamping latihan gym. Kebijakan ini menjelaskan data apa yang kami kumpulkan, bagaimana digunakan, dan hak kamu atas data tersebut.

Data yang kami kumpulkan

Sebagian besar data disimpan lokal di perangkatmu. Jika kamu login (Google Sign-In), sebagian data di bawah ini juga disimpan di server kami agar bisa dipulihkan di perangkat lain:

  • Data akun: email, nama, dan foto profil dari akun Google kamu (via Google Sign-In).
  • Data profil & tubuh: usia, gender, tinggi, berat badan, lokasi latihan, alat yang tersedia.
  • Data kesehatan yang kamu isi sendiri: jawaban PAR-Q (skrining keselamatan sebelum olahraga), riwayat cedera, level aktivitas, hasil tes kapasitas fisik (push-up, plank, sit-to-stand).
  • Data latihan: plan latihan, riwayat sesi, set/rep/beban yang kamu catat, RPE.
  • Data makan (jika memakai Meal Plan): preferensi makan, target kalori/makro, rencana makan, catatan makan.
  • Riwayat chat dengan AI: pesan yang kamu kirim ke fitur Chat AI, disimpan agar percakapan punya konteks dan riwayat.
  • Data teknis: log error/crash (via Sentry) untuk membantu kami memperbaiki bug. Log ini tidak sengaja menyertakan kata sandi atau token akses.

Bagaimana data digunakan

  • Menjalankan fitur inti: plan latihan, PT Mode, riwayat, Assessment, Meal Plan.
  • Chat AI, AI Custom Plan, dan Meal Plan mengirim sebagian data profil/riwayat kamu (secukupnya, bukan seluruh riwayat) ke penyedia model AI pihak ketiga untuk menghasilkan jawaban/plan yang personal. Pesan chat dan permintaan generate plan diproses oleh penyedia tersebut hanya untuk menjawab permintaanmu.
  • Sinkronisasi antar perangkat saat kamu login.
  • Memperbaiki bug lewat laporan crash.

Dengan siapa data dibagikan

  • Supabase — penyedia database & autentikasi tempat data cloud kami disimpan.
  • Google — untuk proses Sign-In.
  • Penyedia model AI — memproses pesan chat dan permintaan generate plan/meal plan.
  • Sentry — menerima log crash/error teknis.

Kami tidak menjual data kamu ke pihak ketiga, dan tidak menampilkan iklan pihak ketiga di aplikasi.

Lokal vs Cloud

Tanpa login, seluruh fitur inti (assessment, plan, PT Mode, library) berjalan 100% lokal di perangkatmu — tidak ada data yang dikirim ke server kami selain untuk fitur yang memang butuh internet (Chat AI, AI Custom Plan, Meal Plan). Login bersifat opsional.

Penyimpanan & keamanan

Data cloud disimpan terenkripsi saat transit (HTTPS) dan di database kami. Akses ke data kamu dibatasi hanya untuk akun kamu sendiri (row-level security).

Berapa lama data disimpan

Data disimpan selama akun kamu aktif. Jika kamu menghapus akun, data cloud terkait dihapus dalam waktu maksimal 30 hari — lihat halaman hapus akun.

Anak-anak

NuFit AI tidak ditujukan untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun, dan kami tidak sengaja mengumpulkan data dari anak di bawah usia tersebut.

Hak kamu

Kamu bisa melihat, mengubah, atau menghapus data profil kapan saja lewat aplikasi, dan menghapus akun beserta data cloud lewat aplikasi atau halaman ini.

Perubahan kebijakan

Jika kebijakan ini berubah secara signifikan, kami akan memperbarui tanggal "Berlaku mulai" di atas.

NuFit AI ("we") is a gym training companion app. This policy explains what data we collect, how it's used, and your rights over it.

Data we collect

Most data stays local on your device. If you sign in (Google Sign-In), some of the data below is also stored on our servers so it can be restored on another device:

  • Account data: email, name, and profile photo from your Google account (via Google Sign-In).
  • Profile & body data: age, gender, height, weight, training location, available equipment.
  • Self-reported health data: PAR-Q answers (pre-exercise safety screening), injury history, activity level, physical capacity test results (push-up, plank, sit-to-stand).
  • Training data: workout plans, session history, the sets/reps/weight you log, RPE.
  • Meal data (if you use Meal Plan): meal preferences, calorie/macro targets, planned meals, meal logs.
  • Chat history with AI: messages you send to the Chat AI feature, kept so the conversation has context and history.
  • Technical data: error/crash logs (via Sentry) to help us fix bugs. These logs do not intentionally include passwords or access tokens.

How data is used

  • Running core features: workout plans, PT Mode, history, Assessment, Meal Plan.
  • Chat AI, AI Custom Plan, and Meal Plan send a limited slice of your profile/history (not your entire history) to a third-party AI model provider to generate personalized answers/plans. Chat messages and plan-generation requests are processed by that provider only to answer your request.
  • Syncing across devices when you're signed in.
  • Fixing bugs through crash reports.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase — the database & authentication provider where our cloud data is stored.
  • Google — for the Sign-In process.
  • AI model provider — processes chat messages and plan/meal-plan generation requests.
  • Sentry — receives technical crash/error logs.

We never sell your data to third parties, and the app shows no third-party ads.

Local vs. Cloud

Without signing in, every core feature (assessment, plan, PT Mode, library) runs 100% locally on your device — nothing is sent to our servers except for features that genuinely need the internet (Chat AI, AI Custom Plan, Meal Plan). Signing in is optional.

Storage & security

Cloud data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest in our database. Access to your data is restricted to your own account (row-level security).

How long we keep data

Data is kept while your account is active. If you delete your account, related cloud data is deleted within 30 days — see the delete account page.

Children

NuFit AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Your rights

You can view, change, or delete your profile data at any time in the app, and delete your account and cloud data via the app or this page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes significantly, we'll update the "Effective" date above.
